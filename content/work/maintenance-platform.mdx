## The problem

Preventive maintenance across the floor didn't have a system of record. The
relationships involved — equipment, maintenance tasks, schedules, and the
teams responsible for them — form a genuinely nested hierarchy, and nobody
had modeled it end to end before.

## What I built

Full-stack ownership: a relational schema with a four-level many-to-many
hierarchy, a REST contract designed and coordinated across frontend,
backend, and DBA teams, and the React client on top of it.

## Outcome

In use across two sites, covering roughly 30% of manufacturing. That's
adoption scope, not measured impact — I don't track how many teams consume
it, so I'm not citing that number.

## What I'd do differently

I'd push harder up front to log actual adoption and usage against the
schema, so "roughly 30%" could eventually become a number backed by
telemetry instead of a scope estimate.
